EV Planner Web

Made by Mohammad Youssef · github.com/mjyousse

A browser version of the EV Planner iPhone app. It plans DC fast-charging stops from a physics-based consumption model, your car's charging curve, terrain, forecast wind and temperature, and posted speed limits.

Proof of concept: consumption is modeled, not measured. Live OBD data is only in the iPhone app.

Data sources

  • Chargers: NREL Alternative Fuels Data Center
  • Routing: OSRM · Search: Photon (OpenStreetMap)
  • Weather & elevation: Open-Meteo
  • Speed limits: OpenStreetMap via Overpass
  • Map: OpenFreeMap · © OpenStreetMap contributors

Prices are estimates by network (guest / non-member rates) unless a station lists its own price. Always confirm in the network's app.
